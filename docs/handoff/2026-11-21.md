### Known deployment issue — left deliberately

The existing MySQL volume has **107 applied migrations whose recorded checksums
differ from the current manifest**. The current files match the committed
manifest (`opengrc:migrations:verify --files-only` passes in the container), so
this is history from an earlier checkout, not the new image.

Container doctor: `19 pass, 2 warn, 1 fail`. Warnings are two privileged accounts
without 2FA and one test evidence source. The failure is the migration-integrity
check above.

Decision taken: **leave the drift for now.** The clean fix is to recreate the
disposable dev DB volume; do not rewrite integrity checksums without an ADR.

## Open items, stated plainly

1. **Dev DB migration checksum drift.** 107 old applied checksums differ from the
   current files. Recreate the dev volume or reconcile deliberately; do not
   silently rewrite the table.
2. **Assistant writes are not built.** The catalogue is read-only. Human-approved
   writes need an approval object, actor, diff and rollback, with their own ADR.
3. **Assistant questions are a fixed catalogue.** A new question is a reviewed
   query and a test, not a prompt edit. Unmatched questions are refused.
4. **DLP is partial.** No first-class policy object, no suppression/exception
   workflow with expiry, no exact-data-match or document fingerprinting, and no
   measured recall. The quality page measures false positives only.
5. **DLP findings do not link to risks/controls.** The risk handoff is manual.
6. **Architecture page is current-state only.** Target state, deployment
   location, roadmap and “deliberately not implemented” are document/risk records,
   not database fields.
7. **Access rules are platform-wide.** No per-OAuth-client or per-application
   lists; email-domain rules act after authentication for panels; CLI account
   creation applies email rules but not IP rules.
8. **Reference status matters.** Managed Sentinel is 2019/CSF 1.1-era; AIDA is
   archived and was proposed, not enacted; the CCCS career guide is 2022 and is a
   career guide, not a competency framework.
9. **No production promotion.** The deployment is the local Compose stack. A
   production deploy needs a backup, the supported install/upgrade path, MFA
   enrolment for privileged accounts, and a fresh doctor.
10. **Streaming monitoring is still not built.** Connectors remain scheduled and
    write evidence after the fact.
