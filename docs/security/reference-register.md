# Security and risk reference register

External sources used by the security, risk and DLP/DSPM skills. Record the
status and the limit of each; a source is not a control and an archived page is
not current law.

| Source | Date / status | Use | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| [NIST SP 800-207, Zero Trust Architecture](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-207) | Final, August 2020 | Zero-trust principles, policy engine / administrator / enforcement point, continuous verification, migration models | Architecture guidance, not a product checklist or a maturity score |
| [NIST SP 1500-4r2, Big Data Interoperability Framework, Volume 4: Security and Privacy](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.1500-4r2) | Final, October 2019 | Data governance, provenance, access, encryption, privacy, audit across large data estates | Big-data framing; not a current control catalogue |
| [Managed Sentinel, Cybersecurity Architecture & Roadmap](https://www.managedsentinel.com/cybersecurity-architecture/) | 2019, MIT licence | One-page current/future architecture by location and NIST CSF function; roadmap and deliberately-not-implemented lanes | NIST CSF 1.1 era; cloud-native, identity-first, AI, supply-chain and data-security controls are under-represented |
| [CCCS, Cyber security career guide](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/cyber-security-career-guide) | 2022 | Four role families for training personas: Govern and support; Protect and defend; Design and develop; Operate and maintain | Career guide, not a competency framework; some statistics are dated (2020–2021) |
| [ISED, AIDA companion document](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovation-better-canada/en/artificial-intelligence-and-data-act-aida-companion-document) | Archived; AIDA was proposed in Bill C-27 and is **not enacted law** | Design input for high-impact AI obligations: human oversight, transparency, fairness, safety, accountability, validity/robustness, monitoring, notification, record-keeping | Do not claim compliance with AIDA. Verify the current legislative status with counsel |

## Rules for using a source

- Cite the identifier and version, not a paraphrase presented as the source.
- Check whether the page is archived, proposed or superseded before relying on it.
- Do not copy licensed standard text into the product, training material or a
  report. Identifiers and mappings are enough.
- Separate architecture guidance from a control catalogue and from law.
- A source that is older than the current framework version may still be useful
  for structure; say which part is dated.
