# Trademarks

"OpenGRC" and the OpenGRC logo are trademarks of OpenGRC, LLC.

The AGPL grants copyright permissions. It does not grant trademark rights.

## What you may do

- State that your product or service is "based on OpenGRC" or "uses OpenGRC".
- Keep the name in the source code, package names, and documentation when you
  redistribute it.
- Use the name in factual, descriptive statements, reviews, and comparisons.

## What you may not do

- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement, partnership, or
  official status without written permission.
- Rename a modified distribution in a way that implies it is the official
  OpenGRC, or ship a modified version under the OpenGRC name without marking the
  changes.
- Register a confusingly similar name, domain, or social account.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the AGPL. A fork that changes behaviour should be clearly
identified as a modified version, for example:

> AcmeGRC — a fork of OpenGRC

and should keep the copyright notices and license intact.
